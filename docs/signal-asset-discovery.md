# Reviewed Pixel and Dataset discovery candidate

`LUMIN_SIGNAL_ASSET_DISCOVERY_ENABLED=false` is the default. Enabling it requires an immutable Marketing USER OAuth profile with `datasetRootIds: { businessId, accountId }`, exact READ actions, no tasks and the reviewed discovery/Dataset/Pixel read capability pack. These are Lumin's conservative requirements, not a claim about Meta's universal minimum permissions. Actual consent, external-client access eligibility and client Business owner binding remain separate gates.

The account and Business must already be registered independently in the exact client/app/provider. The account remains parentless; its independently reviewed owner Business binding is checked separately. OAuth verifies actual `/me/businesses`, Business `/owned_ad_accounts` and account `business` receipts. It enrolls only these two existing roots. It does not enroll a Pixel or Dataset.

`meta_discover_signal_assets` performs bounded read-only inspection. Pixel discovery uses the documented account `adspixels` edge and exact `owner_business` receipt. Dataset discovery uses Business `ads_dataset`, requires `is_consolidated_container=true`, exact `owner_business` and `owner_ad_account`, and requires wrapper `id` to equal `dataset_id`. There is no guessed account Dataset endpoint and no Pixel-to-Dataset conversion. Unknown mappings, incomplete pagination, duplicate identities and foreign ownership receipts fail closed or are withheld.

Transport/schema sources are the official Python Business SDK 26.0.2, pinned at `efd8423a2e595ea8d4c04eb824ce113f2f1d68cd`: [AdAccount](https://github.com/facebook/facebook-python-business-sdk/blob/efd8423a2e595ea8d4c04eb824ce113f2f1d68cd/facebook_business/adobjects/adaccount.py), [Business](https://github.com/facebook/facebook-python-business-sdk/blob/efd8423a2e595ea8d4c04eb824ce113f2f1d68cd/facebook_business/adobjects/business.py), [AdsDataset](https://github.com/facebook/facebook-python-business-sdk/blob/efd8423a2e595ea8d4c04eb824ce113f2f1d68cd/facebook_business/adobjects/adsdataset.py), and [AdsPixel](https://github.com/facebook/facebook-python-business-sdk/blob/efd8423a2e595ea8d4c04eb824ce113f2f1d68cd/facebook_business/adobjects/adspixel.py). Generated schema is not proof of a particular client's live authorization or legal ownership.

Discovery stores an encrypted, identity-bound proposal with at most twenty assets and a five-minute maximum lifetime. An authenticated administrator reviews the exact names and IDs at `/platform/signals/discovery/:proposalId`. A same-origin CSRF-protected browser submission performs one atomic local enrollment, with current membership, global operator index, actual app-user privacy/lifecycle locks, selected encrypted credential, owner evidence and original authorization records checked again. The model has no enrollment or consent setter.

New children have both separately registered Business and account parents. Their grants contain only READ and the exact Dataset metadata or Pixel detail capability, with empty tasks. No event sending, tracking installation, financial authority or write capability is created. Existing same-client assets can be attached only with exact immutable parents and identity; foreign or ambiguous assets are denied.

Supervised refresh reinspects the current Business/account receipts and every registered child through the same typed edges. It renews actual access clocks only after complete matching receipts. It does not extend human eligibility, legal ownership, billing observations, consent duration, credential expiry or event-write authority. The outer lifecycle independently verifies current token app, subject, scopes and locks. There are no automatic provider writes.

Proposals receive a five-minute TTL and personal provenance cleanup. TTL is retention management, not immediate erasure. Local tests and fake-provider receipts do not establish live Meta acceptance. Staging requires frozen full checks, independent review, reviewed registry/index/TTL deployment plans and explicit owner approval; production remains unchanged by this engineering candidate.
