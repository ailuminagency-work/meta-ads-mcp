# Client onboarding and ownership

Status: candidate procedure; no production registry bootstrap or live client acceptance is implied.

Preserve client-owned businesses, ad accounts and billing. Lumin receives authorized partner/task access. A readable account or a valid ads_management token does not prove ownership, billing arrangement, manage role or current external-client eligibility.

1. Establish server-owned tenant/operator index, active membership roles and authorized client record through a reviewed migration.
2. Independently verify client-owned business and asset IDs. Register provider/app-bound roots and complete inspected descendants/reference closure. Record official ownership/billing provenance and client verifiedBusinessIds; never infer this from account names or reads.
3. Configure only a supported immutable deployment OAuth profile with its exact callback and named provider secret reference. Use /platform/connections and select the authorized client. Unsupported profiles remain unavailable; no token-upload MCP shortcut exists.
4. Complete provider consent personally. The adapter independently inspects actual app, subject, type, exact scope grants, expiry and requested root identity; commit encrypted credentials/grants/audit atomically.
5. Refresh trusted access/manage-role and required task evidence without fabricating scopes, ownership or tasks. OAuth enrollment alone leaves product eligibility unverified.
6. Verify isolation, revocation and bounded READ behavior. For minimal paused Ads creation, establish fresh client-owned account/billing and full parent/creative closure/compatibility evidence, enable deployment write policy only when authorized, prepare the exact action with meta_prepare_action, and approve its protected browser page.
7. Execute one approved creation attempt. Its requested status is PAUSED; effective status remains unverified until separately read. New asset/grant/provenance/audit commits atomically. Reconcile UNKNOWN/PARTIAL outcomes before any subsequent step; do not retry or chain automatically.

A successful account read does not establish client-owned billing. Keep real client/account observations in private operational evidence outside this public repository, and resolve any ownership mismatch before campaign construction.

Before rollout, separately authorize the migration manifest and provider configuration, verify App Review/access tier/publication and external-client consent, configure durable indexes/TTL/refresh supervision/monitoring, deploy staging and observe the real end-to-end journey. Fake fixture success is not owner acceptance or provider proof.
