# Optional paid research jobs

This runtime is engineering preparation only. `LUMIN_PAID_RESEARCH_ENABLED=false` is the default, the deployment-owned adapter registry is empty, no vendor is selected, and no paid provider calls have been made. The existing USD50 Google Cloud approval does not authorize research spending.

Seven dedicated MCP tools prepare, run, read status, refresh, retrieve results request abort and perform current-client-administrator financial reconciliation. Preparation records a durable HOLD when disabled/unconfigured. A configured adapter must explicitly guarantee free quotes/status/idempotency lookups, hard per-job cost ceilings, idempotency keys and no additional abort charge. Credentials and adapter registration are deployment-owned; MCP cannot choose credentials or register a provider.

The authenticated `/platform/research/review/:client/:job` page shows the exact query, provenance, provider and concrete quote. A current approver/admin confirms the job ceiling and separate client monthly USD budget. Signed same-origin CSRF binds the reviewed job revision and plan; replay is rejected. This page records approval and never starts a provider.

Run consumes the short-lived approval and reserves the whole quoted ceiling atomically against the same-client monthly ledger before one provider attempt. Concurrent jobs cannot oversubscribe that ledger. Lost receipts become UNKNOWN, keep funds reserved and never automatically resend. A verified free idempotency lookup can recover a provider job ID; an absent/uncertain lookup requires reconciliation and does not release funds.

Abort before a provider attempt costs zero. An accepted provider abort is not final cost proof: the reservation remains until a free actual terminal status reports a bounded charge. Settlement releases only the reservation and adds the observed charge once. There is no automatic paid retry, automatic vendor selection or claimed provider refund.

Requests and normalized results are encrypted with tenant/client/app/job-bound authenticated data. Results contain only bounded normalized items, hashed source references and explicit PUBLIC or CLIENT_PRIVATE provenance; they are not raw vendor payloads. Membership/client/privacy-lock checks run before disclosure and persistence. Research jobs and approvals are personal provenance records subject to app-user privacy handling and7day TTL. A separate shared-client `researchFinancialCheckpoints` record contains hash-keyed scope/idempotency, cost ceiling and an encrypted recoverable opaque provider job reference; these values are not all hashes. It contains no actor, raw job ID, query or result. Unresolved checkpoints have no TTL, and any monthly ledger with reserved funds also has no TTL. Personal job TTL/privacy deletion never cancels provider work or releases money. Current same-client administrators can perform only verified free status/idempotency reconciliation from that checkpoint, without the deleted query, results or original operator. Actual terminal cost settles escrow once. Settled checkpoints expire after90days; unreserved ledgers retain their bounded90day policy. Configure TTL on researchJobs, researchApprovals, researchBudgets and researchFinancialCheckpoints collection groups using expireTime; these are tenant subcollections beneath platform_v1. TTL cleanup is asynchronous and is not evidence that a vendor job was cancelled or refunded.

Synthetic tests cover real service-issued human approval, CSRF/replay rejection, concurrent budget reservations, single attempts, UNKNOWN retention, cancellation reconciliation, cross-client denial and forged storage writes. They do not establish live vendor availability, coverage, compliance, actual credentials or paid-service acceptance.
