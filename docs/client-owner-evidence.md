# Client-owned account and billing evidence

V1 keeps two separate records. A client-owner billing attestation records a human's review of an external evidence artifact, its SHA-256 digest, the registered client business, the exact ad account, reviewer and a fixed expiry of at most90 days. An encrypted Meta account-owner observation records the actual `business.id` from a fixed authorized account read and expires within five minutes. The [Ad Account reference](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/reference/ad-account) describes `business` as the owning Business Manager when one exists. It does not establish payment-method, credit-line or invoice-payment ownership.

With platform discovery enabled in an approved environment, the authenticated connection administrator opens `/platform/ownership/{client_id}/{account_key}`. The browser shows the exact client and ad account and requires an explicit evidence hash, registered client business, validity period and confirmation. No raw owner document, payment details or credential is uploaded. The signed review binds the authenticated principal, account revision and five-minute review window. The first atomic commit changes the account revision, so replay and concurrent acceptance of the same review fail. Same-admin review is allowed; this is a separate human action, not a two-person requirement.

After that review, `meta_observe_account_owner` uses a selected currently authorized User or System User connection with `ads_read`, an exact account READ grant and current Marketing read eligibility. It performs only `GET /act_ACCOUNT?fields=id,account_id,business{id}`. An owner mismatch, revoked connection, changed member or final authorization change prevents the observation from being committed. A global app-user lifecycle lock is checked in the commit transaction. No Meta mutation occurs.

The encrypted observation is a fact about one exact tenant/client/app/account and its registered owner, rather than transferable credential authority. Its originating operator and connection remain audit provenance. A separate selected mutation connection must still authorize every target and ancestor, scope and Page task. Financial approval snapshots bind the exact attestation and observation records, their revisions and their expiry. Changing either record invalidates authorization; a fresh read cannot extend the human attestation's expiry. The old `billingOwnershipVerified` boolean is insufficient to authorize financial changes.

Record retention uses explicit `expireTime` fields and reviewed Firestore TTL plans. TTL is asynchronous cleanup, not a claim of immediate deletion. The deployment package remains disabled until staging approval and provider configuration. Fixture tests establish local engineering behavior only; a real external-client staging journey must independently prove access and owner acceptance.
