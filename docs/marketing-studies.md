# Classic split studies

`marketing_create_classic_split_study` is a default-off engineering candidate (`LUMIN_MARKETING_STUDIES_ENABLED=false`). No app configuration, consent, provider mutation or deployment was performed for this slice.

It creates a classic `SPLIT_TEST` on an explicitly registered Marketing Business root. The human-approved definition selects two existing, currently PAUSED campaigns in one client-owned account and fixes treatment percentages to50/50. Times use Unix seconds: start is between one hour and30days ahead; duration is one hour to7days. These bounds and the conservative USER permission pack (`ads_read`, `ads_management`, `business_management`, plus reviewed permission dependencies) are Lumin restrictions, not claims about minimum Meta permissions or guaranteed study eligibility.

The [current primary split-testing guide](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/guides/split-testing) documents classic Business `/ad_studies` cells referencing campaigns. `SPLIT_TEST_V2`, creative test config, budgets, viewers, clones, creation templates and activation requests are excluded. Creating a study schedules future audience allocation and can have delivery consequences; separate human approval must review that. Sending no activation request does not guarantee Meta has no side effects. The returned study ID proves neither completion nor significance nor a business outcome.

The deployment-owned OAuth profile selects four existing registry roots through `studyRootIds`: one Business, one account and two campaign IDs. Other campaigns in the client registry remain outside that connection. It grants only this study capability and the narrow account/campaign metadata reads, with READ and WRITE_AD_CONFIGURATION actions and no Page tasks. Changes to the deployment profile invalidate the challenge hash. The model cannot choose enrollment roots or expand this scope.

The account remains parentless so ordinary Marketing connections continue to work. The study Business is a separate explicit root, bound to the account's recorded owner Business ID. Actual same-app USER consent, Business membership, owned-account membership, account Business identity and campaign account/status receipts are inspected. These reads prove access/relationship only. Current client owner evidence (or explicitly reviewed deployment bootstrap), durable human billing attestation and fresh actual account ownership observation independently gate the write.

Every create has a separate exact human approval, final authority checks and one durable attempt. Malformed/error-marked receipts or lost responses remain UNKNOWN and never resend automatically. Provider IDs do not enroll extra assets. Local completion registers only the exact returned STUDY under the already approved Business/account/campaign closure and records its immutable approved definition.

Tests use actual adapters, protected local human services and the native MCP SDK with synthetic providers. They do not establish live Meta eligibility, App Review, study success or production readiness. Supervised root continuity must inspect actual token consent and the same bound Business/account/campaign relationships; historical STUDY records must not gain renewed authority without an independently verified study readback contract.
